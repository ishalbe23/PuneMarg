Welcome to PuneMarg

A Pune city navigator where every DSA syllabus topic is a working feature – powered by a hand-written C++ backend.

1

Pick a unit

Use the rail in the middle (or keys 1–7) to choose a syllabus unit, then a feature tab.

2

Choose places

Type a place or simply click it on the map – From, then To. Press Enter to run.

3

Watch it work

Switch to Data structure view to see the stack, queue, heap or tree animate. Step with ← →.
